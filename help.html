How to use it

60° Thread Calc is a shop calculator for single-point 60° threads and three-wire measurement of pitch diameter. The Inch and Metric calculators are independent:

  • INCH 60°THREADS — enter Outside Diameter (inches) and Threads Per Inch.
  • METRIC 60° THREADS — enter Outside Diameter (mm) and Pitch (mm).
  • THREE-WIRE MEASUREMENT — measure pitch diameter over wires; see the section below.

On the Inch and Metric cards, those four cells are the only inputs. Everything else on those two cards is calculated and read-only. Values update as you type. Reset returns the Inch and Metric inputs to 0 and clears the three-wire panel. Print in the header makes a clean shop printout.

What it calculates

For a bolt (external thread): depth with a P/8 flat, and depth for a sharp vee, each in both inches and millimeters. For a nut (internal thread): recommended hole diameter and sharp-vee thread depth, again in both units. The inch side also shows pitch in inches and mm; the metric side shows the equivalent threads per inch.

Thread constants and formulas

Unit conversions use exact 25.4 mm/in (1 / 25.4). Thread factors:

  • 0.6496 — bolt thread depth with a P/8 flat
  • 0.7578 — bolt thread depth, sharp vee
  • 0.5412 — nut-hole calculation: Nut hole dia. = OD − 2 × (0.5412 × pitch)
  • 0.8660 — nut thread depth, sharp vee

If TPI or pitch is zero or negative, pitch/TPI and related depth outputs are shown as 0.

Three-wire measurement

The third card uses the standard 60° three-wire method. Switch Inch (TPI) or Metric (mm) with the segmented toggle. Copy from Inch and Copy from Metric copy outside diameter and TPI or pitch from the other cards and leave Pitch Diameter (E) blank so it can be computed from OD.

  • Enter TPI (inch mode) or Pitch in mm (metric mode). Pitch is also shown in both units.
  • Wire Diameter (W) is optional. Leave it blank to use the best-size wire; the results then show an auto: best hint. If you type a wire diameter greater than zero, that size is used instead.
  • Enter Pitch Diameter (E) directly, or leave it blank and enter Outside Diameter. When E is blank and OD is greater than zero, the basic pitch diameter is computed as E = OD − 0.64951905 × P and marked from OD.

Best-size wire and measurement over wires:

W = 0.57735 × P (exactly P / √3)

M = E + 3W − 0.86603 × P

At the best-size wire this simplifies to M = E + 0.86603 × P. The card reports best wire diameter, the wire diameter used, pitch diameter E, and over-wire measurement M, each in inches and millimeters.

Under Check Measured Over Wires, enter a measured M (greater than zero) to get the actual pitch diameter:

E = M − 3W + 0.86603 × P

Unit conversions use exact 25.4 mm/in.
